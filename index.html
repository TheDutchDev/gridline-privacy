Gridline Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Android and iOS game Gridline (package com.avacadoostudios.gridline), made by Avacadoo Studios, collects and why. If you have questions, email avacadoostudios@gmail.com.

Data the game itself stores

Gridline has no accounts and no sign-in. Your progress, scores, missions, settings and purchases made with in-game currency are saved only on your device. We do not receive or store this data on any server. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Gridline shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, AdMob can collect:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and the approximate location it gives,
  • device information, such as the model, operating system and language,
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data such as crash logs of the ads SDK.

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings, Privacy options.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your phone's settings (Android: Settings, Privacy, Ads). On iOS the game does not ask for tracking permission, so ads there are not personalised.

Payments

The game does not sell anything for real money at the moment. If it does in the future, payments will be handled by Google Play or the App Store; we will not see your card or bank details.

Children

Gridline is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your data. Apart from the ad data described above, which Google collects directly, no data leaves your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
